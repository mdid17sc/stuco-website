Who We Are →

Hi! We're the MDID 17th Student Council, a group of students elected to represent every voice at our school. We work with classmates, teachers, and administrators to make MDID a place where everyone feels included and excited to be here.

From planning events and spirit weeks to leading service projects, we turn student ideas into action. Have a suggestion or concern? We're here to listen.
