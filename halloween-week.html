Schedule

  • Homeroom Decorating Competition

    Decorate: Oct 12–23 · Judging: Oct 27–29

    Grades 7–11 classes turn their rooms into the most haunted room in school. The top 3 classes win prizes.

  • Spooky Ladyfingers Decoration

    Oct 30 · 12:40–13:10 · 10F Study Area

    Decorate your own ladyfingers. Sign-up required.

  • Horror Movie Showcase

    Oct 27–28 · 12:20–13:10 · 6-7 Performing Arts Classroom

    A mini movie theatre at lunch with popcorn. Sign-up required.

  • Haunted Hallway

    Oct 27–30 · 6F Hallway

    Walk through our Harry Potter, Coraline, and Stranger Things themed spooky hallway. Please don't touch or break the decorations!

  • Trick or Treat Stations

    Oct 27–30 · 5F–10F

    Hunt for little buckets of treats hidden around the school. They move each day.

  • Halloween Catwalk

    Oct 29 · 15:10–16:10 · Fu Lai Hall (2F)

    Show off your best costume on stage. You can walk the red carpet on your own or with your friends!
