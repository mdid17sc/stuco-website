About this pillar

Students are happier when the little things are easy, like having accessible umbrellas on a rainy day, or a clean trash area after lunchtime. We're rolling out initiatives to cut down on everyday hassles and keep our campus clean and comfortable, so students can spend their time and energy on what matters to them.
