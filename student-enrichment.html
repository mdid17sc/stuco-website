Student Enrichment

About this pillar

We believe education builds the next generation of leaders and skilled individuals who will carry MDID forward. We want to open student-led workshops and skills-based programs to help students grow, and keep department facilities open until 7:00 PM so everyone has extra time to collaborate and work on personal projects.

  • +Image

    Enrichment Programs

    Explore interests, improve leadership skills, and stand out on your college applications through opening your own after-school clubs (especially for 10th graders who only have one club per week)!

  • +Image

    Extended Campus Hours

    Keeping our department open until 7:00 PM.
