4th Pillar:
Feedback Systems

A platform for you to express concern & suggest improvements to us.

About this pillar

Our feedback system is a sophisticated google form system where everyone is welcomed to provide ideas about our inventory, equipment, campus, event/activities, and vending machine items; Reports about our facilities, maintenance, or environmental sanitation; Or feedback about events, activities and even vending machine snacks!

Every thought of yours is a crucial element that builds up to our MDID community, therefore feel free to reach out whenever you need to via the google forms attached below!

    Feedback

    Provide feedback and thoughts on school events.

  • +Image

    Report

    Report any missing or broken equipment in school!

  • +Image

    Ideas

    Provide ideas and suggestions for our future events to make school more fun!
